# Pool Seasonal Care & Extreme Weather Skill

Use this skill to guide opening, winterizing closing, storm prep, and extreme weather management.

## 1. Post-Rainstorm & Storm Recovery
- **Rain Dilution Effect**: Heavy rain lowers Total Alkalinity, Calcium Hardness, and Cyanuric Acid, and introduces airborne nitrates and organic debris.
- **Protocol**:
  1. Pump down excess water if above mid-skimmer.
  2. Clear skimmer and pump baskets.
  3. Run pump 24 hours continuously.
  4. Shock pool with 3-5 ppm extra liquid chlorine to oxidize organic contaminants.
  5. Test and adjust pH (rain is often slightly acidic, pH 5.5 - 6.5).

## 2. Summer Heatwave & Algae Prevention
- High water temperatures (> 85°F / 30°C) accelerate chlorine degradation and bacterial proliferation.
- **Action**:
  - Maintain Free Chlorine at high end of target range (3.5 - 5.0 ppm).
  - Check CYA is at least 40 ppm for outdoor sun exposure.
  - Increase pump daily runtime by 2 - 4 hours.
  - Brush dead spots, corners, and behind ladder steps daily.

## 3. Spring Pool Opening Checklist
1. Remove, clean, and dry winter safety cover.
2. Remove winterizing freeze plugs from return lines and skimmers; reinstall directional eyeballs and skimmer baskets.
3. Reinstall drain plugs in pump, filter, heater, and chlorinator.
4. Fill pool to mid-skimmer level.
5. Prime pump, inspect for leaks, and start filtration.
6. Test water parameters and balance in order: Alkalinity $\rightarrow$ pH $\rightarrow$ Calcium Hardness $\rightarrow$ Stabilizer $\rightarrow$ Chlorine Shock.
