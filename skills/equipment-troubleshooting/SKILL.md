# Pool Equipment Troubleshooting Skill

Use this skill when diagnosing equipment malfunctions, abnormal pressures, air leaks, or water flow issues.

## 1. Filter Pressure & Flow Diagnostics
- **Clean Baseline Pressure**: Note filter pressure when media (sand, cartridge, DE) is clean.
- **Backwash / Rinse Rule**: When pressure rises **8 - 10 PSI above clean baseline**, immediate backwash (sand/DE) or cartridge cleaning is required.
- **Low Filter Pressure (< baseline)**:
  - Cause: Suction side restriction, clogged skimmer basket, clogged pump strainer, closed suction valve, low pool water level causing vortex air intake.
- **High Filter Pressure (> baseline + 10 PSI)**:
  - Cause: Dirty filter media, closed return valve, clogged return eyeballs, calcified heater exchanger.

## 2. Pump Issues
- **Pump not priming / Air bubbles under pump lid**:
  - Check pump lid O-ring (lubricate with silicone/teflon lube, replace if cracked).
  - Check drain plugs on pump housing.
  - Check union couplings on suction line.
  - Check water level at skimmer weir door.
- **Loud screeching noise**:
  - Motor bearings worn out due to shaft seal failure.
- **Loud gravel/rock rattling noise**:
  - Pump cavitation due to severe suction restriction or oversized impeller.

## 3. Salt Chlorine Generator (SWG) Troubleshooting
- **Low Salt / Check Cell warning**:
  - Test actual water salt level with salinity drops/meter.
  - Inspect cell plates for white calcium scale. Soak in 4:1 water/muriatic acid solution for 10-15 minutes if scaled.
  - Check cell temperature sensor in cold water (< 60°F / 15°C SWGs shut off).

## 4. Robotic / Suction Cleaners
- Cleaner stalling: Clean dedicated inline filter, inspect tracks/wheels, verify vacuum suction regulator setting.
